01High risk of losing money

Trading foreign exchange (forex), contracts for difference (CFDs), indices, gold, cryptocurrencies and other leveraged products carries a high level of risk and is not suitable for everyone. Most retail traders lose money. You can lose all of the money you put in, and with some products and brokers you can lose more than your initial deposit.

02Leverage works both ways

Leverage lets you control a large position with a small deposit. A small price move against you can therefore cause a large loss, trigger a margin call or close your positions automatically. Always know your position size and stop loss before you enter a trade.

03Education, not advice

1 Percent Club teaches how markets work and how to build and test your own trading plan. Nothing we publish is a recommendation to trade, a signal service or personal advice. We are not licensed by the Securities Commission Malaysia to give investment advice, and we do not manage money. Every trading decision is yours.

04Past results do not repeat

Examples, charts, backtests, case studies and student results show what happened in the past, often in specific market conditions. They do not mean you will get the same results. Hypothetical and backtested results have limits: they are made with hindsight and do not include every real-world cost, delay and emotion.

05Indicators can be wrong

Our indicators and alerts describe price behaviour on a chart. They can give false or late readings, behave differently on different platforms, brokers and timeframes, and fail in fast or thin markets. Test any tool on a demo account first.

06Crypto assets

Cryptocurrencies are highly volatile and may not be regulated where you live. In Malaysia, only digital asset exchanges registered with the Securities Commission may operate legally. Check that any exchange or broker you use is licensed by your local regulator.

07Only trade what you can afford to lose

Never trade with money you need for living costs, debts or emergencies, and never borrow to trade. If you are unsure whether trading is right for you, speak to a licensed financial adviser.
