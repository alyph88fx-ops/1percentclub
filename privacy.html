Privacy Policy

What personal data 1 Percent Club collects, why, who it is shared with and how to reach us about it. Written in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

01Who is responsible

The data user (controller) is 1 Percent Club ("1 Percent Club", "we", "us" or "our"), a trading education brand operated from Malaysia by its founder. For any question or request about your personal data, email hello@1-percentclub.com.

02What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the list or create a free account.
  • Account and membership details, if you become a member: your name, email, plan, billing history and support messages.
  • Technical data that any website receives: your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you request. Our hosting provider records this in short-lived server logs.
  • Preferences stored on your device: the member desk saves a few settings (such as reminders and ticked milestones) in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

We do not collect payment card numbers on this website, and we do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

03Why we use it

  • To send you what you signed up for: the free Foundations track, membership emails and occasional news about new lessons and live sessions.
  • To run your membership, answer support requests and keep records we are required to keep.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We use your data only for these purposes, or for a directly related purpose, and with your consent where the law requires it. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or using the link in the email.

04Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run 1 Percent Club, and only what they need:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Gmail) stores the email list and our email.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code libraries to your browser, so they receive your IP address when a page loads.
  • A payment provider, if you buy a membership. It handles your card details directly; we never see them.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

05Transfers outside Malaysia

These providers store data on servers outside Malaysia, mainly in the United States. We use providers that protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA, as permitted by section 129 of the Act.

06How long we keep it

We keep your email on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Membership and billing records are kept for as long as you are a member and then for up to 7 years where tax and accounting rules require it. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short time only.

07Your rights

Under the PDPA you can:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong, incomplete or out of date;
  • withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop sending you marketing;
  • ask us to delete your data, unless we must keep it by law;
  • ask us to send your data to another organisation in a common format.

Email hello@1-percentclub.com from the address you signed up with. We will reply within 21 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia.

08Security

We limit who can access personal data, use providers with strong security, and serve the website only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. If a data breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify you and the Commissioner as the law requires.

09Children

1 Percent Club is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced by email or on the member desk.

11Language

This policy is published in English. A Bahasa Malaysia version is available on request; if the two differ, the Bahasa Malaysia version applies to the extent the PDPA requires.
